Before you write to us

Where is my order?

A tracking link is emailed as soon as your parcel is collected. If it hasn’t moved for three business days, write to us with your order number and we’ll chase the carrier.

Can I return earbuds or in-ear monitors once opened?

Yes. We replace the ear tips on every returned pair, so opened in-ear products are covered by the same 30-day policy.

Which model works with my console or amplifier?

Compatibility is listed under Specifications on every product page. For studio models, the impedance tells you how much amplification you need — 80 Ω and below is comfortable from a laptop or interface.

Do you replace batteries?

Yes. Battery replacement for any wireless model costs $39 including return shipping, and takes about a week.

Is this a real shop?

No — Auren is a portfolio concept. The cart and checkout work in your browser, but nothing can be bought and no data is sent anywhere.
