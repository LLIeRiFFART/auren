    Six ways to listen

    View all models

      Precision in every layer

      Every part of the earcup earns its place. Take it apart, one layer at a time.

      1. Machined from a single billet, then spun-finished so light travels around it. The whole surface is a touch control for volume, tracks and calls.

      2. An anodised ring carries eight microphones behind micro-perforations. They sample the world 48,000 times a second for noise cancelling and clear calls.

      3. A glass-reinforced composite shell forms a tuned 32 cc acoustic chamber. Its mass and inner ribbing keep resonance out of the music.

      4. The audio processor, a 32-bit DAC and a 680 mAh cell in each cup. Sixty hours with noise cancelling on; five minutes of charge returns four hours.

      5. A beryllium-coated diaphragm on a free-edge surround, driven by a neodymium magnet. Stiff, light and fast: 4 Hz to 40 kHz with under 0.1% distortion.

      6. A damped baffle angles the driver towards the ear canal. Its woven mesh evens out airflow and protects the diaphragm.

      7. Slow-rebound foam in protein leather seals without pressure. Cushions attach magnetically, so replacing them takes seconds, not tools.
